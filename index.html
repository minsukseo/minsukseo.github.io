I am a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC) in the Materials Science and Engineering department, formerly at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory. I hold a PhD in Nuclear Engineering from Penn State University, where I conducted experimental research on advanced materials characterization.

My research focuses on understanding the structure and properties of two-dimensional materials using advanced analytical techniques. I have extensive experience with electron microscopy and surface analysis methods, with a strong background in both experimental design and data analysis.
